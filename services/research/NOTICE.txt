Vertex research package

This package is first-party Vertex code. Its features, setups, options,
portfolio and replay modules were first written in Gamma Forge, the owner's
earlier version of Vertex. They are native Vertex building blocks; Gamma Forge
is not an upstream project and is not tracked as one. Git history keeps the
original import.

No Fincept, TradingAgents, quant-mind, provider client, provider dataset, corpus,
credential, historical worksheet, order or execution implementation is copied.
Provider names and ticker/company labels in characterization tests are
synthetic test metadata, not fetched data or evidence of provider entitlement.

Pydantic, pydantic-core, tzdata and test/build tools remain separately installed
dependencies under their own distribution notices. They are not vendored or
relicensed by this package. Preserve their installed distribution notices.
