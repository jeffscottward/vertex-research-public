## Part 1: Mathematical and Financial Correctness (`D1`–`D10`)

- **D1 (High) — Market State Atlas `BUILDABLE_FREE` contradicts `L2` rolling Tape history & lacks causal filter:** Under `$0` proxy `L2`, intraday Tape has zero history on Day 1; historical analog search also lacks causal guard ($i \le t - \max(h)$) and minimum sample quorum ($\ge 5$). **PROPOSE FIX:** Define Dual-Mode `Tape-EOD` (daily survivorship-free inputs back to 1995+) for Day-1 `$0` Atlas with strict causal filter and $\ge 5$-episode quorum; label intraday `Tape-Live` separately.
- **D2 (High) — DTCC SBSR 5Y CDS naive median mixes SNAC upfront points, running coupons, package trades & tight-IG noise:** Single-name US corporate CDS trade on 100/500 bps fixed coupons + upfront points, IMM 4.5–5.5Y tenors, and sparse daily prints. **PROPOSE FIX:** Filter `Action Type = NEWT` (net `CANC`/`CORR`), non-package single-name `[4.5, 5.5]Y`; normalize upfront/par spreads via documented ISDA/risky-PV01 or restrict to par-quoted `Basis points` with drop-rate logging; use 5-day rolling Winsorized median ($\ge 3$ trades) and require both `>= +25%` AND `>= +15 bps` (baseline `>= 25 bps`).
- **D3 (High) — Hidden Bear Index sign inversion & undefined composite:** Subtracting negative drawdowns (`-15% - (-3%) = -12%`) inverts sign. **PROPOSE FIX:** Define non-negative drawdown magnitude $\text{DDMag}_{52w}(X)_t = 1 - C_t / \max_{[t-251,t]} C_i \in [0, 1]$ so $\text{HBI\_Gap}_t = \text{DDMag}(\text{RSP}) - \text{DDMag}(\text{SPY}) > 0$ when equal-weight lags cap-weight, with companion constituent diagnostics.
- **D4 (High) — ETF flow `NAV_t` vs `NAV_{t-1}` lag, reverse splits & impossible Form N-PORT backfill for 1933 Act Gold ETFs:** Issuer share updates reflect T+1 settlement; 3x inverse ETFs reverse-split frequently; physical gold ETFs (`GLD`, `IAU`, `SGOL`, `BAR`, `GLDM`, `AAAU`) are 1933 Act grantor trusts that never file Form N-PORT. **PROPOSE FIX:** Multiply share delta by `NAV_{t-1}` (verified per issuer); adjust splits via corporate actions + AUM-continuity check; replace N-PORT gold claim with SSGA `GLD`/WGC historical archives + 10-Q/10-K shares, noting N-PORT's 60d lag for 1940 Act funds.
- **D5 (Medium) — Hindenburg Omen missing `USI:UNCH` denominator, `2.2%` vs `2.8%`, and `SPY` vs `^NYA`:** Omitting unchanged issues shrinks $N_t$ by 2–5% and triggers false positives; `^NYA` is free on Yahoo. **PROPOSE FIX:** Collect `USI:UNCH` / WSJ Unchanged so $N_t = \text{ADV} + \text{DECL} + \text{UNCH}$, ingest `^NYA`, lock Condition 4 to `McOsc_RANA < 0`, and document `2.2%` classic vs `2.8%` common-stock threshold.
- **D6 (Medium) — McClellan Summation anchor & Zweig Breadth Thrust disarm semantics:** EMA seed, Summation base (`0` vs `1000`), and Zweig re-arming/cooldown rules unspecified. **PROPOSE FIX:** Pin standard EMA smoothing ($\alpha_{19}=0.10$, $\alpha_{39}=0.05$, SMA-seeded), Summation base `0` (documented), and explicit Zweig state machine (`<=0.40` arms; `>=0.615` within 10 sessions fires and immediately disarms).
- **D7 (Medium) — Titanic Syndrome causal window, dividend-adjusted high, and 10d A-D slope:** `"within 7 sessions"` could imply look-ahead; ex-dividend drops skew `SPY` highs; slope metric undefined. **PROPOSE FIX:** Lock strictly causal `[t-7, t]` window on `^GSPC` (or split-only `SPY`), define `10d A-D slope` explicitly (`AD_t < AD_{t-10}`), and use $N_t = \text{ADV}+\text{DECL}+\text{UNCH}$.
- **D8 (Medium) — BPI `2%` log box vs classic dollar-step, persistent state machine, and survivorship bias:** `2%` log box is slower (`6.12%` reversal) than Dorsey Wright dollar-step; BPI tracks persistent buy/sell state, not same-day breakouts; backfilling on today's `SPY` holdings injects survivorship bias. **PROPOSE FIX:** Specify persistent High/Low 3-box P&F state machine (`Log 2%` labeled explicitly) and restrict historical constituent replays to forward `constituent_snapshots` only (never static-constituent backfills).
- **D9 (Medium) — Valuation Clock overclaims "zero look-ahead" on revised FRED vintages and omits BEA/Shiller lags:** `fredgraph.csv` serves revised data (not ALFRED vintages); BEA `CP` (+90d Q4), `GDP` (+35d), and Shiller CAPE (+45d) lags were omitted; `XLK` cold-starts in 1998. **PROPOSE FIX:** Reword to *"release-lagged expanding-window replay (revised FRED vintages; ALFRED out of scope)"*, publish full lag table (`Z.1 +75d`, `GDP +35d`, `CP +90d`, `FINRA +25d`, `Shiller +45d`, `CPI +15d`), and require `>=10y` warm-up.
- **D10 (Medium) — SPY Triggers, Coppock, Fear & Greed, and Volatility Outlook precision:** `20d` cooldown breaks `Monthly Faber` and re-fires `10% Correction`; Coppock ROC units & intra-month freeze undefined; Fear & Greed lacks bounds/quorum/smoothed UVOL; Vol Outlook conflates min–max scaling with empirical percentile rank (`VIXEQ` launched Nov 2023). **PROPOSE FIX:** Use new-ATH reset for `10% Correction`, no daily cooldown on `Monthly Faber`, total-return vs price-return note + unconditional base rate; percent Coppock frozen on month-end; concrete 8-component Fear & Greed bounds + `>=6/8` quorum + 10d EMA UVOL; rolling 1,260-session empirical percentile rank for Vol Outlook with `<5y` sample note for `VIXEQ`.

## Part 2: Upstream Free Data-Feed Realism (`D11`–`D16`)

- **D11 (High) — SEC EDGAR `companyfacts` strips XBRL dimensional ad segments, has broken PK, and tag/multi-class drift:** `companyfacts` omits `ProductOrServiceAxis` (cannot supply YouTube/Amazon ad segments); `xbrl_company_quarters` PK omitted `fiscal_year`/`period_end_date` and Q4 subtraction; diluted vs cover-page shares breaks multi-class stocks. **PROPOSE FIX:** Defer SEC XBRL fundamentals to Tier-2 Expansion, fix PK to `(cik, period_end_date, form_type, filed_date)`, specify Q4 = `FY - (Q1+Q2+Q3)`, require Inline XBRL (`_htm.xml`) for ad-segment axes, and use `dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding` summed across share classes.
- **D12 (High) — TradingView unauthenticated WS scaled to 23 sessions, missing `$TICK` High/Low, and `MMTW`/`MMFI`/`MMTH` fragility:** `tradingview.ts` stores only `{ time, close }`, dropping `$TICK` intraday ±1,000 spikes; 23 simultaneous sessions risk throttling; WSJ cannot backstop 5m `$TICK` or `MMTW`. **PROPOSE FIX:** Treat `MMTW`/`MMFI`/`MMTH` as optional overlay (compute constituent `% > MA` directly), capture OHLC `{ open, high, low, close }` for `TICK`/`TICKQ`, cap ≤6–8 sessions/socket (or read IBKR `TICK-NYSE`/`AD-NYSE`), and fix fallback claims in §7.
- **D13 (High) — Yahoo Finance 750-symbol throttling, non-viable IBKR equity fallback, and `^MOVE` outages:** IBKR HMDS pacing (~60 req/10 min) cannot backstop 750 equities; `^MOVE` freezes periodically on Yahoo. **PROPOSE FIX:** Chunk Yahoo batch fetches (`<=50` tickers/req + jitter) with **Stooq** daily CSV as equity fallback (IBKR strictly for `<=30` core ETFs/futures); auto-fallback `^MOVE` to `TLT` 30d IV inside Volatility Outlook without stalling the panel.
- **D14 (High) — DTCC SBSR lacks UPI/ISIN/RED/LEI-to-issuer mapping and file-volume budget:** Daily SBSR credit files identify entities via UPI/ISIN/RED/LEI, not company names. **PROPOSE FIX:** Add a GLEIF LEI / ANNA-DSB UPI reference-mapping lookup table and isolate `dtcc_sbsr.py` as a Tier-2 non-gating job with stream-unzip processing.
- **D15 (Medium) — Issuer ETF CSV/XLSX schema drift & missing issuers for 23 Cash / 9 Gold ETFs:** Six named issuers do not cover Vanguard/JPMorgan/PIMCO/Goldman/abrdn/GraniteShares/Sprott; SSGA serves `.xlsx`, Invesco gates bots. **PROPOSE FIX:** Narrow `$0` core flows to verified stable issuers (>85% category AUM, visibly labeled as subset) with strict parser invariants (`openpyxl`, header/row-count/weight-sum `[98%, 102%]`) failing non-gatingly.
- **D16 (Medium) — OCC endpoint & account-type limits + mandatory `EIA_API_KEY` & `FRED_API_KEY`:** OCC reports cleared contract counts (not buy/sell or dollar premium); EIA v2 requires a free registered `EIA_API_KEY`; FRED release calendar requires `FRED_API_KEY`; `series.py` needs `>=250 ms` pacing for ~100 series. **PROPOSE FIX:** Separate OCC cleared volume from QuantData dollar premium; document required free `EIA_API_KEY` & `FRED_API_KEY`; add polite pacing + `--since` watermarks in `series.py`.
